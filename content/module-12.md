# Module 12: Licences and Statutory Compliance

A hospital needs many government licences, and NABH checks that each one is valid on the day of assessment. You already handle some of these. This module lists the common ones, tells you which hospitals need each, and shows where each sits in the NABH chapters.

### Why NABH cares about licences

ROM.2 asks the hospital to run within the law, and other chapters check the licences for specific services. An expired licence is a non-conformity, and a missing licence for a service the hospital provides can stop certification. NABH recommends a licence tracker so that no renewal is missed.

### Licences needed by every hospital

| Licence | Issued by | NABH chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clinical establishment registration | State health department, usually through the CMO office | ROM |
| Fire NOC | State fire service | FMS |
| Biomedical waste authorisation | State Pollution Control Board | IPC |
| Consent to Establish and Consent to Operate (Water and Air Acts) | State Pollution Control Board | FMS |
| Agreement with a CBWTF (waste vendor) | Contract with an authorised facility | IPC |
| Building completion or occupancy certificate | Local development authority or municipal body | FMS |
| Electrical safety inspection and lift licence | State electrical inspector and lift inspector | FMS |
| Trade or municipal licence | Municipal corporation | ROM |
| Food licence (FSSAI) for the hospital kitchen | Food safety department | IPC |
| Labour registrations: Shops and Establishments, ESI, PF | Labour department, ESIC, EPFO | HRM |
| Registration of every doctor and nurse | Medical council and nursing council | HRM |

### Licences needed only for certain services

| Service | Licence | Issued by | NABH chapter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pharmacy | Retail drug licence | State drug controller | MOM |
| Narcotic medicines such as morphine | NDPS licence for possession and use | State excise or drug department | MOM |
| X-ray, CT, cath lab | AERB registration or licence | Atomic Energy Regulatory Board | AAC |
| Ultrasound | PCPNDT registration | District appropriate authority, usually the CMO | AAC |
| Abortion services | MTP approval | District level committee | COP |
| Blood bank or blood storage centre | Blood bank licence | State drug controller and CDSCO | COP |
| Laboratory | Registration under the clinical establishment rules | State health department | AAC |
| Ambulance | Vehicle registration and permit | RTO | COP |
| Organ transplant | Registration under the Transplantation of Human Organs Act | State appropriate authority | COP |
| Use of rectified spirit | Spirit licence | State excise department | MOM |
| Generator set | Pollution clearance for the DG set | State Pollution Control Board | FMS |

The exact name and issuing office of each licence differs from state to state. Always check the state's current rules before advising a client.

### The licence tracker

Keep one tracker for each client hospital, with these columns:

| Column | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Licence name | As printed on the certificate |
| Licence number | As printed |
| Issued by | Department and office |
| Date of issue | From the certificate |
| Valid until | From the certificate, or one-time if there is no expiry |
| Renewal due | A date well before expiry, since renewals take time |
| Status | Valid, applied for renewal, expired or not applicable |
| Copy filed | Where the scanned and physical copies are kept |
| Responsible person | Who in the hospital follows up |

### Points assessors check

- The name and address on every licence match the hospital's current name and address.
- The bed count on the registration matches the beds actually in use.
- Services listed on the scope of services board are covered by licences.
- Renewal applications are filed before expiry, with the acknowledgement kept as proof.
- Licences are displayed where the law requires, for example the PCPNDT certificate in the ultrasound room.

### Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Statutory | Required by law |
| CMO | Chief Medical Officer of the district |
| NOC | No Objection Certificate |
| SPCB | State Pollution Control Board |
| NDPS | Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act |
| AERB | Atomic Energy Regulatory Board |
| PCPNDT | Pre-Conception and Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques Act, which governs ultrasound to prevent sex selection |
| MTP | Medical Termination of Pregnancy |
| CDSCO | Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation |
