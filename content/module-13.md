# Module 13: TPA, Insurance and NABH

NABH status affects how much a hospital is paid and how easily its claims pass. The records that NABH asks a hospital to keep are the same records that insurers check before paying. This module joins your TPA work to everything you have learnt so far.

### The parties in a cashless claim

| Party | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Patient or policyholder | Holds the health insurance policy |
| Insurer | The insurance company that pays the claim |
| TPA | Third Party Administrator, which processes claims for the insurer |
| Hospital TPA desk | Sends requests and documents, follows up queries |
| Network or empanelled hospital | A hospital that has an agreement with the insurer or TPA for cashless treatment |

For PSU insurers (New India, United India, Oriental and National), empanelment and rates are agreed through the PPN (Preferred Provider Network) arrangement.

### How NABH changes empanelment and rates

- **Government schemes:** under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY, an empanelled hospital with NABH Entry Level certification is paid 10 percent above the standard package rate, and one with full NABH accreditation is paid 15 percent above it.
- **Private and PSU insurers:** insurers commonly look at NABH status when deciding whether to empanel a hospital and what tariff to agree. Accredited hospitals are generally in a stronger position to negotiate rates.
- **Patient choice:** insured patients and corporate clients often prefer accredited hospitals.

When you send an empanelment application, attach the NABH certificate if the hospital has one, along with the licences from Module 12.

### The claim journey

1. **Pre-authorisation.** At admission the TPA desk sends a request with the doctor's notes on the diagnosis, the planned treatment, the estimated cost and the patient's ID and policy card.
2. **Approval.** The TPA approves an initial amount or raises a query.
3. **Enhancement.** If the treatment changes or costs more, the desk asks for an enhanced amount with the reasons.
4. **Final approval.** At discharge the final bill and discharge summary are sent for final approval.
5. **Claim submission.** The full set of documents goes to the TPA.
6. **Settlement.** The TPA settles the claim, deducts non-payable items, or raises a query.

### Documents in a claim and where they come from

| Document | What the insurer checks | NABH chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admission notes and initial assessment | Was admission needed? Do the complaints match the diagnosis? | AAC.3 |
| Doctor's progress notes | Was each day of stay justified? | AAC.4 |
| Investigation reports | Do the reports support the diagnosis? | AAC.5, AAC.6 |
| Medication chart | Were the billed medicines ordered and given? | MOM.5 |
| Operation and anaesthesia notes | What was done, by whom and when? | COP.7, COP.8 |
| Implant sticker and invoice | Was the billed implant actually used? | MOM.7 |
| Consent forms | Was the patient informed? | PRE.2 |
| Discharge summary | Final diagnosis, dates, procedure and condition at discharge | AAC.7 |
| Final bill with breakup | Are charges as per the agreed tariff and room category? | ROM.2 |
| MLC papers, where applicable | Is there a police intimation for an accident case? | COP.2 |

### Common query reasons, and the NABH fix

| Query from TPA | Usual cause | NABH area that prevents it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diagnosis in the discharge summary does not match the case sheet | Discharge summary written in a hurry | IMS.2 record audits |
| Dates of admission or surgery differ between documents | Manual entries not checked | IMS.2 record audits |
| Medicine billed but not on the medication chart | Charting gaps | MOM.5 |
| Need for admission not justified | Thin initial assessment | AAC.3 |
| Implant sticker missing | Sticker not pasted in the file | MOM.7 |
| Missing signatures or unsigned consent | No signature check before discharge | PRE.2, IMS.2 |
| Illegible notes | Handwriting, no format | IMS.2 |
| Room rent above the policy limit | Room category not checked at admission | AAC.2 |

A hospital with a working NABH system has fewer of these queries, because the records are complete before the file reaches the desk.

### Your checklist before sending a claim

- [ ] Patient name, UHID and IP number are the same on every page
- [ ] Dates of admission, surgery and discharge match across all documents
- [ ] Final diagnosis matches between the discharge summary, case sheet and pre-authorisation
- [ ] Discharge summary is signed with the doctor's name and registration number
- [ ] All consents are signed and dated
- [ ] Every medicine and consumable billed appears in the doctor's orders and the medication chart
- [ ] Implant stickers and invoices are attached
- [ ] Investigation reports cited in the summary are attached
- [ ] Bill follows the agreed tariff and room category
- [ ] MLC copy attached for accident and injury cases

### Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Empanelment | Agreement that makes a hospital part of an insurer's network |
| PPN | Preferred Provider Network of the PSU insurers |
| PM-JAY | Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana, the government health insurance scheme |
| Package rate | Fixed price for a procedure, including stay, medicines and tests |
| Enhancement | Request to increase the approved amount |
| Deduction | Amount the insurer refuses to pay from the bill |
| Non-payable items | Items not covered by the policy, such as some consumables |
| Query | Question raised by the TPA before it settles a claim |
